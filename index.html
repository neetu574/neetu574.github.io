About

I'm a student in the Department of Non-Formal and Lifelong Education at the Institute of Education and Research (IER), University of Dhaka. My academic interests sit at the intersection of non-formal education theory, digital literacy, and the ways social media can be used as a genuine educational tool.

Outside the classroom, I teach, create content, and edit video and photo — three things that all come back to the same idea: making learning and stories easier to reach.
